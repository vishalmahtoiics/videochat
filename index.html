Family Locate

See a family member's phone location, only when you ask.

Open parent dashboard

Install the Android app on the phone you want to locate, set this site's address and a private key, then come back to the dashboard and tap Check location.

Prefer the browser instead of the app? Open the share page on that phone.

Location is shared only when requested, and the phone always shows a notification while sharing is on. Intended for your own device, or a child's device with their knowledge.
